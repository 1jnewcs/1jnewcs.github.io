Technical Leader Rigs

Beaverhead Dry-Dropper Rig

Tailwater Standard

Designed for deep undercut banks and swift water. Keeps dry fly afloat while heavy sowbug/nymph sinks rapidly.

Leader: 9ft 4X Tapered Fluorocarbon

Dry Fly: Foam Salmonfly or Sofa Pillow (#6-10) attached via Improved Clinch

Tippet: 24"-36" 5X Fluorocarbon tied to hook bend via Non-Slip Loop or Clinch

Dropper Nymph: Gut Sack Sow Bug or Jig Twisted Caddis (#14-18)

Tactical Euro / Tight-Line Nymph Rig

High Precision

Maximum depth control with micro-drag prevention. Ideal for heavy tungsten tailwater nymphs.

Euro Leader: 15ft 0X Monofilament Butt + 18" Two-Tone Sighter Line

Tippet Ring: 2mm Micro Ring attached to sighter

Point Fly: Heavy Tungsten Jig Twisted Caddis (#12-14) on 5X fluorocarbon

Tag Fly: 6" Tag 20" above point: Higa's SOS or Juan's Bling Midge (#16-20)

Essential Fly Fishing Knots Guide

1. Improved Clinch Knot

Use: Securing fly to tippet (up to 12lb).

  1. Pass tag end through hook eye.
  2. Wrap tag end around standing line 5-7 times.
  3. Pass tag through first loop next to hook eye.
  4. Pass tag back through large outer loop.
  5. Moisten line and pull tight smoothly.

2. Non-Slip Mono Loop

Use: Free movement for streamers & nymphs.

  1. Make an overhand knot 2 inches from end of line.
  2. Pass tag end through hook eye and back through overhand loop.
  3. Wrap tag around standing line 4 times.
  4. Pass tag back through overhand loop in direction entered.
  5. Moisten and pull tag to seat loop.

3. Triple Surgeon’s Knot

Use: Joining leader to tippet (unequal diameters).

  1. Overlap leader and tippet ends by 6 inches.
  2. Form simple loop with both strands together.
  3. Pass tag end and entire tippet strand through loop 3 times.
  4. Moisten knot and pull all 4 ends steadily to tighten.
  5. Trim tag ends flush.

4. Blood Knot

Use: Joining two lines of similar diameter.

  1. Overlap two line ends by 6 inches.
  2. Wrap Line A around Line B 5 times; turn tag back to center gap.
  3. Wrap Line B around Line A 5 times in opposite direction.
  4. Pass Line B tag through center gap opposite to Line A tag.
  5. Moisten and pull standing lines in opposite directions.

5. Davy Knot

Use: Fast, compact knot for small midges/nymphs.

  1. Pass tag through hook eye (3 to 4 inches).
  2. Form simple overhand loop around standing line.
  3. Bring tag back over loop and through loop center.
  4. Tighten by pulling standing line first, then tag.

6. Uni Knot

Use: Strong, versatile line-to-hook or loop connection.

  1. Run tag through hook eye and double back to create a loop.
  2. Wrap tag end around double line 4 to 5 times inside loop.
  3. Moisten line and pull tag to snug down wraps.
  4. Slide knot down firmly against hook eye.
